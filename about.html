About SourceSetu

Your Independent B2B Sourcing Partner

We aim to help business buyers explore suitable product sourcing options based on their requirements.

What we do

Start with the requirement. Build from there.

SourceSetu is designed as an independent B2B sourcing and supply partner. We help retailers, wholesalers, distributors, shopkeepers and other business buyers communicate what they need and explore sourcing options.

We do not present SourceSetu as a manufacturer or claim that products are sold under a proprietary private label. Product specifications, supplier details, MOQ, availability, pricing and timelines need to be checked and confirmed for each enquiry.

Our working principles

  • Understand the product brief before proposing options.
  • Keep pricing quotation-based rather than publishing unverified prices.
  • Clarify product, payment, quality and delivery terms before order confirmation.
  • Be transparent when details are still pending confirmation.
