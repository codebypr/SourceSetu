Our process

From Requirement to Quotation

Clear steps to help both sides review product and commercial details before a commitment is made.

1

Share Your Requirements

Tell us the product, specifications, quantity, target timeline and delivery city/state.

2

We Review Sourcing Options

Potential options are explored according to your brief and feasibility.

3

Receive a Quotation

Review the proposed product details, price, MOQ where confirmed, taxes where applicable and delivery terms.

4

Confirm Supply Terms

Order processing begins only after both sides agree on the applicable terms.

Before confirmation

Know what is being offered

Ask for written confirmation of specifications, quality expectations, quantity, price, applicable taxes, payment schedule, dispatch and delivery terms before placing an order.
